## What bathroom remodeling do you do?

We do anything from a tub-to-shower swap to a full bathroom remodel. That covers new tile, new fixtures, new flooring, drywall, and paint. We also fix the rotted subfloor and wet walls that old bathrooms often hide. You get one written price before any work starts.

Here is what a bathroom job can cover.

| Job | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| Tub-to-shower swap | Old tub out, walk-in shower in |
| Tile | Shower walls and floors |
| Fixtures | New fixtures to fit your layout |
| Flooring | Tile and vinyl plank |
| Walls and ceiling | Drywall, patching, and paint |
| Water damage | Rotted subfloor and wet framing fixed |

## Why swap a tub for a walk-in shower?

A walk-in shower is easier and safer to step into than a tub. It is easier to clean, and it often makes a small bathroom feel bigger. If no one in your house uses the tub, a shower puts that space to work.

We talk through the size, the layout, and the tile with you before we price it.

## What drives the price of a bathroom remodel?

Four things move the number most: how much you change, the tile and fixtures you pick, whether plumbing has to move, and what we find under the old floor. Old bathrooms can hide water damage under a tub or toilet, and it has to be fixed before the new floor goes in.

Here is what changes the price:

- **Scope.** A shower swap costs less than a full remodel.
- **Materials.** Large tile and custom work take more time.
- **Plumbing.** Moving a drain or a toilet adds work.
- **Hidden damage.** Rotted subfloor gets replaced.

We do not quote remodels over the phone. We look first.

## How does a bathroom remodel with you work?

It starts with a visit to look at your bathroom and hear what you want. You get one written price. Then one crew does the job and cleans up as we go. We tell you before we start how long the bathroom will be out of use, so you can plan.
