## What kitchen remodeling work do you do?

We update cabinets, counters, flooring, and lighting. We hang and finish drywall, paint walls and trim, and fix water damage under the sink or behind the walls. Jobs run from a fresh look on a few things to a full kitchen remodel. You get a written price before we start.

Here is what a kitchen job can cover.

| Part of the kitchen | What we do |
| --- | --- |
| Cabinets | Update or replace them |
| Counters | New counters to fit your layout |
| Flooring | Laminate, vinyl plank, and tile |
| Lighting | New fixtures and better light on the work areas |
| Walls and ceiling | Drywall repair, patching, and paint |
| Water damage | Rotted subfloor and wet walls fixed |

## How does a kitchen remodel with you work?

It starts with a visit. We look at your kitchen and you tell us what you want and what bugs you about it now. We give you one written price. Then one crew does the work and cleans up as we go. You know who is in your house every day.

We talk through the schedule before we start, so you know how long the kitchen will be out of use.

## What drives the price of a kitchen remodel?

Four things move the number most: how much you change, the materials you pick, the size of the room, and what we find behind the old cabinets and floor. Old kitchens can hide water damage under the sink, and it has to be fixed before new work goes in.

Here is what changes the price:

- **Scope.** New cabinets and counters cost more than updates.
- **Materials.** Tile and solid counters cost more than laminate.
- **Layout.** Moving plumbing or walls adds work.
- **Hidden damage.** Rotted subfloor gets replaced.

We do not quote remodels over the phone. We look first.

## Can you fix a kitchen after a leak?

Yes. A roof or plumbing leak can ruin a ceiling, the drywall, and the floor below. We fix the wet wood and framing, put up new drywall, paint, and replace the flooring. If the leak came from the roof, our roofing crew stops it first.
