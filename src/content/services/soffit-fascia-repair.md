## What do soffit and fascia do?

Fascia is the board along the edge of your roof that holds your gutters. Soffit is the panel under the overhang. Together they close off the roof edge so rain, birds, squirrels, and wasps stay out. Vented soffit also pulls fresh air into your attic, which helps your shingles last.

Here is what each one does.

| Part | Where it is | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fascia | Along the roof edge | Holds the gutters and seals the edge |
| Soffit | Under the overhang | Closes the gap to your wall |
| Soffit vents | In the soffit | Let air into the attic |
| Drip edge | On top of the fascia | Sends rain into the gutter |

## How do I know my soffit or fascia needs repair?

Look for paint peeling off the board behind your gutters, wood that feels soft, and gutters pulling away from the house. Holes, sagging soffit, and wasp or bird nests under the overhang are signs too. Any of these means the roof edge is open.

Squirrels love a soft spot in a soffit. Once they get into an attic, they chew wiring and insulation.

## Why does soffit and fascia damage happen?

Most of it comes from water. When a gutter clogs or pulls loose, rain spills over and soaks the fascia behind it. Storms and falling limbs break boards too. In the Arkansas heat and humidity, wet wood rots fast, so a small soft spot grows every season.

That is why we always check the gutter and the drip edge when we replace a board.
