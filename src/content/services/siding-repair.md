## What kinds of siding damage do you repair?

We repair siding that is cracked, loose, warped, or damaged by hail, wind, or a fallen limb. We also fix the trim and the soft spots where water got behind a board. The goal is simple: seal the wall again so rain and wind stay out of your house.

Here is what we see most around Hot Springs.

| What you see | What usually causes it | The usual fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cracked or holed boards | Hail or a thrown rock | Replace the damaged boards |
| Loose or flapping panels | High wind | Refasten or replace the section |
| Warped vinyl | Heat, often from a grill or reflection | Replace the warped boards |
| Soft spots you can press in | Water behind the siding | Fix the wood, then the siding |
| Gaps at windows and trim | Old caulk and worn trim | Reseal and repair the trim |

## How do you repair siding?

We pull off the damaged boards and look at the wall behind them before anything goes back on. If the sheathing is wet or soft, we fix that first. Then we put in new boards that match as close as we can and seal the edges. You see the finished wall before we leave.

Skipping the look behind the siding is how a small repair turns into a big one later.

## Why fix siding damage right away?

A crack or a loose panel lets rain get behind the siding. In Arkansas humidity, that wet wood does not dry out. It rots, and it can grow mold inside the wall. A repair now keeps the fix small. Waiting can turn it into new sheathing and a whole new wall.

Watch for paint that will not hold, stains on inside walls, and boards that feel soft.

## Can you repair metal siding?

Yes. We work on metal siding as well as vinyl and fiber cement. Our crew wraps shops and outbuildings in metal and installs metal siding on homes, so we know how the panels fit and fasten. A damaged metal panel can often be swapped without redoing the wall.
