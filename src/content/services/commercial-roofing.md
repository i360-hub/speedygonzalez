## Which roof is right for my building?

It depends on the slope. A flat or low-slope roof needs a membrane, and TPO is the most common pick around Hot Springs. A roof with slope can take metal panels. We look at your building and tell you which one fits.

Here is how the two compare.

| Roof type | How long it lasts | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [TPO membrane](/services/tpo-flat-roofing) | 20–30 years | Flat and low-slope roofs |
| [Metal roof](/services/metal-roofing) | 40–70 years | Roofs with slope |

TPO is white. It reflects the Arkansas sun and keeps your building cooler.

## How much does a commercial roof cost?

It depends on three things: the square footage, what sits on the roof, and whether the old roof comes off first. A small shop prices out very differently than a big roof covered in vents and units. We measure your roof for free and put the number in writing.

Here is what drives the cost:

- **Square footage.** Bigger roof, more material and labor.
- **What is on the roof.** Vents, pipes, and HVAC units each need flashing by hand.
- **Tear-off or overlay.** If the old roof is soaked, it comes off first.

Wet insulation under an old membrane is common. We check for it before we quote.

## Why does my flat roof keep leaking?

Flat roofs leak because water sits on them. Ponding water works into any weak seam, old patch, or worn flashing. Once a membrane is at the end of its life, patching one spot just moves the leak.

Look for these warning signs.

| What you see | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Puddles two days after rain | Bad slope or clogged drain |
| Bubbles or ripples | Water trapped under the membrane |
| Stains on your ceiling | Active leak, act now |

If water is coming in now, our [roof leak repair](/services/roof-leak-repair) crew can find it and stop it.
