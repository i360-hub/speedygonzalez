## Why have a roofer install your skylight?

A skylight is a hole in your roof, so how it is flashed decides whether it leaks. Our crew works on roofs every day. We cut the opening, frame it, and flash the skylight into your shingles or metal so water runs around it. That is the part that matters most.

Here is what goes into a skylight job.

| Step | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Pick the spot | Clear the rafters and get light where you want it |
| Cut and frame | A square, solid opening holds the unit tight |
| Set the skylight | Level and sealed to the frame |
| Flash it in | Water runs around the skylight, not into it |
| Check it | We look over the flashing and seals before we leave |

## Why is my skylight leaking?

Most skylight leaks come from flashing or a seal that failed, not from the glass. Old caulk dries out in the Arkansas sun. Flashing can lift in a storm, and leaves can pile up on the uphill side and push water under the edge. A drip near the skylight is the usual first sign.

A water stain around the skylight inside means water has been getting in for a while. Call before the drywall goes soft.

## Can you repair my skylight or do I need a new one?

We repair skylights when the glass and frame are still good. That usually means new flashing and new seals. We replace skylights that are cracked, fogged between the panes, or sitting in a rotted frame. Either way, we look at it first and tell you which one makes sense.

Hail can crack skylights too. If a storm hit your roof, see [hail damage repair](/services/hail-damage-repair).

## Do you install skylights on metal roofs?

Yes. We work skylights into shingle and metal roofs alike. On metal, the skylight has to fit the panels and the flashing has to match the ribs, so the water still has a clear path down.
