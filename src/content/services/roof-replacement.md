## How do I know my roof needs replacing?

Replace your roof when the wear is everywhere, not in one spot. Shingles curling across the whole roof, granules piling up in your gutters, and a roof past 20 years old all point the same way. A patch on a worn-out roof just moves the leak somewhere else.

Here is how to tell a repair from a replacement.

| What you see | What it means | Repair or replace |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A few shingles gone after wind | Damage in one spot | Repair |
| Curling shingles all over | Shingles at the end of their life | Replace |
| Granules filling your gutters | Shingles wearing out | Replace |
| Roof past 20 years old | Near the end for shingles | Replace soon |
| Sagging roof line | Wood under the roof is failing | Replace |

If the damage is in one area, a [roof repair](/services/roof-repair) is the right call. We tell you which one you have. Read our list of [7 signs you need a new roof](/blog/signs-you-need-a-new-roof).

## What does a roof replacement include?

We tear off your old roof down to the wood. Then we check the decking, replace any rotten boards, and lay new underlayment. The new shingles or metal panels go on next, with new flashing and ridge vents. We haul off the old roof and clean your yard.

Here is the job, step by step:

- **Free inspection.** We get on the roof, take photos, and show you what we find.
- **Written price.** You get one number in writing before we start.
- **Tear-off.** The old roof comes off down to the wood.
- **Decking check.** We replace boards that are rotten.
- **New roof.** Underlayment, shingles or metal, flashing, and vents.
- **Cleanup.** We run a magnet over the grass and driveway for nails.

You get a walk-through before we pull out. If something looks off to you, we fix it then.

## What kind of new roof should I pick?

Most Hot Springs homeowners pick shingles or metal. Shingles cost less up front. Metal lasts two to three times as long and holds up better to hail. Flat and low-slope sections need a membrane instead, because shingles need slope to work.

Here is how the three compare.

| Roof type | How long it lasts | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Shingle](/services/shingle-roofing) | 15–25 years | Lower upfront cost, most homes |
| [Metal](/services/metal-roofing) | 40–70 years | Staying put long term, hail country |
| [TPO flat roof](/services/tpo-flat-roofing) | 20–30 years | Flat and low-slope roofs |

No one roof is right for everyone. We help you weigh them at your inspection.

## How much does a roof replacement cost in Hot Springs?

It depends on four things: the size of your roof, how steep it is, the material you pick, and what we find under the old roof. A simple ranch prices out very differently than a big home with valleys and dormers. We inspect for free and put the number in writing.

Here is what moves your price:

- **Size.** More square feet means more material and more labor.
- **Pitch.** A steep roof is slower work.
- **Shape.** Valleys, chimneys, and skylights need flashing and hand work.
- **What is underneath.** Rotten decking gets replaced.

We do not quote roofs over the phone. Read more in [what a new roof costs in Hot Springs](/blog/roof-replacement-cost-arkansas).

## Will insurance pay to replace my roof?

Insurance may pay if hail or wind damaged your roof. It will not pay for a roof that simply got old. Your policy and your adjuster decide what is covered, not your roofer. We inspect for free, photograph the damage, and meet your adjuster on site.

Arkansas hail season runs March through June, so storm claims are common in Garland County. See [how insurance claims work](/insurance-claims), [hail damage repair](/services/hail-damage-repair), and [storm damage repair](/services/storm-damage-repair).

We never pressure you to file a claim you do not need.
