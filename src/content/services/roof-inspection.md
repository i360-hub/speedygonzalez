## What happens during a roof inspection?

Someone from our own team climbs onto your roof and checks it up close. We look at the shingles or metal, the flashing, the vents, and the valleys. We take photos of every problem we find. Then we sit down with you and go through them.

Here is what we check.

| Part of the roof | What we look for |
| --- | --- |
| Shingles or metal | Hail bruises, cracks, curling, loose panels |
| Flashing | Gaps at chimneys, walls, and skylights |
| Pipe boots | Cracked rubber that lets water in |
| Valleys | Worn spots where two slopes meet |
| Vents | Blocked or damaged attic vents |
| Gutters and fascia | Sagging runs and soft, rotted boards |
| Attic, if you want | Stains, damp wood, and daylight |

You get the photos and a plain answer. Nothing is hidden on the roof where you cannot see it.

## When should I get my roof inspected?

Get your roof checked after any hail or high wind, before you buy or sell a house, and when your roof passes 15 years old. A yearly check is smart too. Small problems found early are cheap to fix. The same problems found late can mean rotten wood.

Signs you should call now:

- **A water stain** on a ceiling or wall
- **Shingles in the yard** after a storm
- **Granules** piling up in your gutters
- **A neighbor** getting a new roof after the same storm

We photograph and mark every hail hit, then help you decide if a claim makes sense. If you file, we meet your adjuster on your roof and show them what we found. Your insurance company decides what it pays, not us. We just make sure the damage gets seen.
