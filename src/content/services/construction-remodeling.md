## What construction and remodeling work do you do?

We remodel kitchens and bathrooms, hang drywall, lay flooring, and paint inside and out. We also fix damaged framing and build new buildings from the ground up. Jobs run from one room to a whole house. You get a written price before we start.

Here is what we handle.

| Job | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| Kitchen remodeling | Cabinets, counters, flooring, and lighting |
| Bathroom remodeling | Tub-to-shower jobs up to a full remodel |
| Drywall | Hung, taped, and finished, ready for paint |
| Flooring | Laminate, vinyl plank, and tile, plus floor repair |
| Painting | Walls, ceilings, and trim inside; siding and trim outside |
| Home renovations | One room or the whole house |
| Structural repairs | Rotted wood, sagging ceilings, damaged framing |
| New buildings | Built from site prep to the roof |

Not sure where your job fits? Call 501-359-5550 and tell us what you want done.

## Do you remodel kitchens and bathrooms?

Yes. In a kitchen we update cabinets, counters, flooring, and lighting. In a bathroom we do anything from a tub-to-shower swap to a full remodel, with new tile and fixtures. One contractor runs your job from the first visit through cleanup.

We talk through what you want before we price it. Then you get one written number.

## Do you do drywall, flooring, and painting?

Yes. We hang, tape, and finish drywall so walls and ceilings are ready for paint. We install laminate, vinyl plank, and tile floors, and we fix floors that are worn or water-damaged. We paint inside and outside.

These jobs often come as a set. A new wall needs paint. A leak that ruins a ceiling often ruins the floor under it too.

Outside, fresh paint protects your [siding](/services/siding), trim, and fascia from Arkansas sun and storms.

## Can you fix the inside of my house after a leak or storm?

Yes. We stop the water first, then fix what it damaged. That means sagging ceilings, wet drywall, water-damaged framing, and ruined floors. One crew handles the roof and the inside, so you do not have to line up a second contractor.

Start with [roof leak repair](/services/roof-leak-repair) or [storm damage repair](/services/storm-damage-repair) if water is still coming in.

If a storm caused it, your insurance may cover the repairs. See [how insurance claims work](/insurance-claims).

## How much does a remodel cost?

It depends on the size of the job, the materials you pick, and what we find behind the walls. A new floor in one room prices out very differently than a full kitchen. We look at the job and give you a written price before we start.

Here is what moves your number:

- **Size.** More rooms and more square feet mean more material and labor.
- **Materials.** Tile, cabinets, and counters come in a wide range.
- **What is hidden.** Rot or water damage behind a wall gets fixed first.

If we open a wall and find something we could not see, we stop and show you before we go on.
