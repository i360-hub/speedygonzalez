## What does your gutter cleaning include?

We scoop out the leaves, twigs, and grit from every gutter run. Then we run water through so we know each downspout drains clear. While we are on the ladder, we check the gutters, the fascia board behind them, and the edge of your roof. You hear about any problem we see.

Here is what we do on a cleaning.

| Step | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| Clear every run | Rain drains instead of spilling over |
| Flush the downspouts | Water leaves the house, not the wall |
| Check for sagging | Loose runs get pointed out before they fall |
| Look at the fascia | Soft wood found early is a small fix |
| Look at the roof edge | Lifted shingles and drip edge get noticed |

## Why have a roofer clean my gutters?

A roofer knows what an overflowing gutter does to a house. When we clean yours, we can also tell you if the fascia is rotting, if a run has pulled loose, or if shingles at the edge are lifting. A cleaning crew may not know what to look for.

If we find a problem, you get photos and a plain answer. Fixing it is your call.

## How do I know my gutters need cleaning?

Watch your gutters in a hard rain. Water pouring over the front edge is the clearest sign. Plants growing out of a gutter, sagging runs, and dirt splashed up on your siding all point the same way. So do puddles right against your foundation after a storm.

Hot Springs has a lot of oaks and pines. Leaves in fall and pollen and needles in spring fill gutters fast.
