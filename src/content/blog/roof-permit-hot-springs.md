## Do you need a permit to replace a roof in Hot Springs?

Inside the city limits, plan on one. The City of Hot Springs issues a residential roof permit, and its monthly permit reports list tear-offs and re-roofs. Outside the city, Garland County generally issues no building permits. Hot Springs Village has its own permit through the property owners' association.

The answer depends on which side of a line your house sits on.

| Where your house is | Permit for a re-roof? | Who to call |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inside Hot Springs city limits | Yes, plan on a city roof permit | City Permits Division, 501-321-6851 |
| Within one mile outside the city | The city may require one | City Permits Division, 501-321-6851 |
| Rest of Garland County | Generally none from the county | Check your covenants |
| Hot Springs Village | Yes, from the POA | POA Permitting, 501-922-5562 |

## What does the city require?

The city treats a re-roof as permitted work. Its permit report dated September 1, 2026 lists 27 residential roof permits. They include full tear-offs, a roof repair, and a tear-off marked as done by the homeowner.

The city's "Do I need a building permit?" page lists the work that is exempt. Roofing is not on that list. The same page says ordinary repairs need no notice, but it does not say where a small roof repair ends and a re-roof begins.

So call before the job, not after. The Permits Division is on the first floor of City Hall at 133 Convention Boulevard.

## What does a roof permit cost?

The fee is set by the value of the job. The schedule is in the city code, and it steps up with the value of the work. On the September 2026 report, roof permit fees came to roughly half of one percent of the value of the work.

Skipping it costs more. The city code says fees are tripled when work starts before the permit is issued.

## Who pulls the permit?

Usually the roofer. On the city's reports, roof permits are listed under the roofing company's name, ours included. Ask any roofer you hire who is pulling the permit, and get the answer in writing.

State law gives roofers a reason to do it right. A roofing registration can be revoked for doing roofing work without a permit that a local authority requires.

## What if you live outside the city limits?

Then the county is not your permit office. Garland County's guideline letter says the county generally does not have building codes and does not issue building permits. It also says the county has no zoning program.

Three things can still apply.

- **The one-mile area.** The county letter says the city may require permits within one mile outside the city limits.
- **Covenants.** A subdivision's bill of assurance can set rules on roof color and material.
- **Floodplain.** The county letter says a floodplain permit is required for any new construction or significant remodeling in the county. The Floodplain Administrator may issue an exemption when no flood hazard area is affected.

## What about Hot Springs Village?

The Village runs its own permits. The property owners' association requires a permit for roofing on existing homes, and its portal lists replacement roofing as a permit type.

Our guide to [roof replacement in Hot Springs Village](/blog/hot-springs-village-roof-replacement-rules) covers the color rule and the townhome rules.

## Can you roof your own house?

State law allows it. The owner of a single-family home is exempt from the roofing registration when acting as their own roofer on their own property. The permit is a separate question, and the city's report shows homeowners pulling roof permits too.

We would still say stay off the roof. Falls are the reason.

## Does the building code change what gets done?

Yes. Hot Springs has adopted the 2021 Arkansas Fire Prevention Code, which includes the 2021 residential code. That code says a roof replacement means taking the old roofing off down to the deck. It limits laying new roofing over old.

For the code's limits on new roofing over old, read [putting a metal roof over shingles](/blog/metal-roof-over-shingles).

For the rule people ask about most, read [the 25% rule and Arkansas](/blog/25-percent-rule-roofing-arkansas).
