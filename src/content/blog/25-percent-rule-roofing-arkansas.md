## What is the 25% rule for roofing?

It is a rule from the Florida Building Code. It said that no more than 25 percent of a roof could be repaired or replaced in any 12-month period unless the whole roof was brought up to the current code. In practice, heavy damage often meant a full replacement.

Florida changed the rule in 2022. For roofs built to the 2007 Florida code or later, only the repaired part now has to meet the current code.

You see the rule in Arkansas search results because so much roofing and insurance writing comes from Florida. It is a real rule. It is just not ours.

## Does the 25% rule apply in Arkansas?

No. Arkansas uses the 2021 International Residential Code for homes, as part of the Arkansas Fire Prevention Code. Its reroofing section has no percentage trigger. Arkansas adopted the roofing chapter with no changes.

The City of Hot Springs has adopted that same 2021 code. We did not find a local amendment that adds a percentage rule. If you are inside the city limits, the city's Permits and Inspections office can confirm it at 501-321-6851.

Here is how the two states compare.

| | Florida | Arkansas |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Percentage rule | Yes, 25 percent, narrowed in 2022 | None in the residential code |
| Where it is written | Florida Building Code and Florida Statutes 553.844 | 2021 International Residential Code, reroofing section |
| What decides a full replacement | The 25 percent test, on older roofs | The condition of the roof and your policy |

## What does the Arkansas code say about roof work?

It sets three plain rules. They apply whether a storm or old age is the reason for the work.

- **A replacement is a tear-off.** The code says a roof replacement includes removing the old roofing down to the deck.
- **Laying new over old is limited.** It is not allowed when the old roof is water soaked or too worn to be a sound base.
- **Two layers is the limit.** New roofing cannot go over a roof that already has two or more layers.

The structure also has to carry the load.

Our guide on [putting a metal roof over shingles](/blog/metal-roof-over-shingles) covers what that means for a metal roof.

## So when does a damaged roof get fully replaced?

When it cannot be repaired to work, or when your policy pays for it. Those are two separate questions, and people mix them up.

The first is a roofing question. A repair works when the damage is in one area and the rest of the roof is sound. It does not work when hail has bruised shingles across every slope, or when the shingles are too brittle to lift and reseal.

The second is an insurance question. Your policy and your adjuster decide what is paid. We cannot promise an outcome, and no roofer can. Arkansas does not allow public adjusters. We document the damage and meet the adjuster. We do not negotiate your claim.

## What should you do if only part of your roof is damaged?

Get every slope looked at, not just the one that leaks. Storm damage is often wider than it looks from the yard.

1. Take photos from the ground and note the date of the storm.
2. Have a roofer inspect and photograph the whole roof.
3. Report the claim promptly. Check your policy for a filing deadline.
4. Meet the adjuster with the roofer's photos in hand.
5. Ask how the policy treats repairs that will not match the rest of the roof.

On that deadline: the Arkansas Insurance Department has said a 365-day limit may be reasonable, and that insurers must allow a good-cause exception. Do not test it. File early.
