## What drives the cost of a metal roof in Arkansas?

Six things. The type of panel, the thickness of the metal, the size of the roof, how steep it is, how cut up it is, and what is under the old roof. Two homes on the same street can price out far apart because of them.

We do not post a price, and we do not quote over the phone. A number without a roof behind it is a guess. Here is what moves the real one.

| What changes the price | Costs less | Costs more |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Panel type | Exposed fastener | Standing seam |
| Metal thickness | Lighter gauge | Heavier gauge |
| Roof size | Smaller | Larger |
| Pitch | Walkable | Steep |
| Shape | One or two planes | Valleys, dormers, chimneys |
| Under the old roof | Sound decking | Rotten wood to replace |

## How much does the panel type matter?

A lot. It is the first choice you make. Exposed fastener panels are screwed through the face of the metal and go on quickly. Standing seam panels hide the fasteners under raised seams and are cut and locked by hand.

Standing seam costs more. It also has no screw holes on the surface, so there is less to maintain as the roof ages. We recommend it for most homes and exposed fastener panels for shops, barns, and porches.

## Why does metal thickness matter?

Thicker metal costs more and takes hail better. A federal energy lab notes that heavier gauge metal resists hail damage better than lighter gauge metal. In Garland County, that is worth paying attention to.

Ask what gauge is in the quote. A lower gauge number means thicker metal. Two quotes that look far apart are sometimes two different thicknesses.

## How do size, pitch, and shape change the price?

Size sets the amount of metal. Pitch and shape set the amount of labor.

- **Size.** More roof means more panels, more trim, and more days.
- **Pitch.** A steep roof is slower to work on.
- **Shape.** Every valley, dormer, chimney, and skylight needs flashing cut and fitted by hand.

Shape matters more for metal than for shingles. A shingle bends around a valley. A metal panel has to be cut to it.

## What about the old roof?

It adds cost in one of two ways. Tearing it off takes labor and a dumpster. Leaving it on saves that, but only a sound, single layer of shingles can be covered.

Then there is the wood. Rotten decking has to be replaced, and no one can see it until the old roof is off. We photograph it before we touch it, so you see what you are paying for. Our guide on [putting a metal roof over shingles](/blog/metal-roof-over-shingles) covers when each approach works.

## Is metal cheaper than shingles in the long run?

It can be. Shingles cost less on the day they go on. Metal lasts two to three times as long. Over 50 years, that is usually one metal roof against two or three shingle roofs.

The math favors metal if you stay in the house. It favors shingles if you plan to sell in a few years. Read [metal vs shingles in Arkansas](/blog/metal-vs-shingles-arkansas) for the full comparison, and [how long a metal roof lasts](/blog/how-long-does-a-metal-roof-last) for what upkeep to expect.

## Can insurance change the math?

Yes. If a storm damaged your roof, your policy may pay to replace it. If you move from shingles to metal on a claim, expect to pay the difference between the two. Confirm that with your adjuster.

Ask your agent two things first. Does a metal roof change my premium? Does my policy treat hail dents on metal as cosmetic? Our guide on [new roofs and home insurance](/blog/new-roof-home-insurance-arkansas) covers the Arkansas rules.
